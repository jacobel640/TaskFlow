# TaskFlow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: <!-- FILL IN: publication date -->_

TaskFlow ("the app") is a task management app. This policy explains which data the app handles and why.

## Using the app without an account
When you continue without an account, all your tasks and settings are stored **only on your device**.
They are not sent to us or to any third party. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the
app's database may be included in your personal Google device backup, under your Google account
and according to Google's terms.

## Using the app with a Google account
If you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses **Firebase Authentication** (Google) to sign you in, and
**Cloud Firestore** (Google) to back up your tasks. In that case the following data is processed:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Name, email address and Google account ID | Signing you in and identifying your backup |
| Task content (title, text, status, priority, dates) | Backing up and restoring your tasks across devices |

- The data is used only to provide the backup and sync feature. It is **not** sold, shared for
  advertising, or used for tracking.
- The data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by Google Cloud in accordance with the
  [Firebase privacy and security terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- Only you can access your backed up tasks. The security rules of the database allow every user to
  read and write only their own tasks.

## Notifications
Daily reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent for that purpose.

## Deleting your data
- **In the app:** Account screen → **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account and every
  task backed up in the cloud. Tasks stored on your device remain there until you delete them or
  uninstall the app.
- **Without the app:** send a deletion request to <!-- FILL IN: contact email --> from the email address of your
  Google account, and the account and its data will be deleted within 30 days.

Signing out stops the backup. It does not delete data that was already backed up.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
